# Privacy Policy for ExifClean

**Effective Date:** October 17, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 17, 2026

ExifClean ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed from the ground up to protect user privacy. We believe your sensitive documents, scans, and metadata belong exclusively to you.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection & Storage
- **No Personal Data Collected:** ExifClean does not collect, log, transmit, or sell any personal information, accounts, emails, or device identifiers.
- **100% On-Device Processing:** Document edge detection, corner cropping, filtering, and PDF generation run entirely on your local device hardware using Google Play Services ML Kit. Scanned documents and images never leave your phone or upload to external servers.

---

### 2. EXIF & Metadata Stripping
ExifClean actively removes sensitive embedded EXIF metadata from scanned documents and exported images/PDFs, including:
- GPS coordinates & location history
- Camera hardware serial numbers, make, and model
- Exposure settings, shutter speeds, and camera timestamps

---

### 3. Permissions Used
ExifClean follows the principle of least privilege:
- **Camera:** Handled securely via Google Play Services Document Scanner. ExifClean itself does not require direct raw camera permissions in the app manifest.
- **Storage / Files:** Used solely to read documents you select from your gallery and to save your exported PDFs/images to your chosen storage or Paperless inbox folder.
- **Biometric / Screen Lock (Optional):** Used locally via Android BiometricPrompt to protect your private document vault. Your biometric data is handled by Android OS security hardware and is never accessible to the app.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services
ExifClean uses standard Google Play Services dependencies for scanning:
- **Google Play Services (ML Kit Document Scanner):** Provides edge detection and perspective correction on-device. For details on Google's privacy practices, refer to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
ExifClean does not address anyone under the age of 13 and collects zero personal information from any user.
